📅 2026-09-15👥 Hayato Futami, Hassan Shahmohammadi, Tushar Dhyani, Alkis Koudounas, Raphaël Lafargue, Yosuke Kashiwagi, Quentin Jodelet, Eileen Long

📅 2026-09-15👥 Yunqi Lu, Tyler Baumgartner, Nikhil Johri, Brandon Tai, Candice Fan, Luc Debaupte, Ruben Aguilar, Bill Wang, Yi Zhong
